# Theme brief — direction, not an approved palette

## Founder feedback

The founder liked the earlier light-mode colour direction but rejected the dark-mode treatment and the regressions in later collages. They requested inspiration from tables in Singapore primary and secondary schools. None of the generated logos, palette assignments or colour meanings is accepted automatically.

This is a design reference request, not a claim that Singapore schools use one standard colour scheme. No specific school/table photograph or sampled palette has been verified in this package. When colour exploration is explicitly requested, use an identified reference or a clearly labelled interpretation; do not invent an official school palette.

## Proposed exploration

Preserve the calm light experience: warm readable surfaces, restrained teal/green accents and modest supporting colour. For dark mode, test neutral warm charcoal, slightly lighter document surfaces and muted furniture-inspired accent colours rather than a highly saturated navy/cyan dashboard. This is a candidate, not an approved hex-token set.

The school-table inspiration should influence material warmth and restrained colour, not turn the interface into a literal classroom illustration, wood-grain wallpaper or rainbow of cards. Avoid neon glow, excessive gradients, glass panels and large coloured status areas. No group/level colour doubles as mastery proof.

## Semantic tokens after approval

Record background, surface, elevated surface, text, muted text, border, focus, selection, accent, source/tutor/learner provenance, warning and error roles. Light/dark modes share components, navigation, data and graph semantics; only intentional visual token values change. Do not create separate independently drifting designs for each theme.

Check normal/large text contrast and non-text controls against the applicable criteria in actual renders. A mockup is not an accessibility pass. Do not rely on colour alone to distinguish authorship, graph level, edge relation or evidence state. [U2, U3]

## Review process

First review one selected surface in one mode with real synthetic content. Preserve its shell and geometry when exploring the other mode. Discuss one candidate at a time and record its accepted/rejected details in VISUAL_SELECTION.md. Do not generate a multi-theme presentation board.
