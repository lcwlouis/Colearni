# Parachutes

## Overview

Parachutes is the debut studio album by Coldplay.

Released in 2000, the album established Coldplay's early sound through intimate songwriting, emotional vulnerability, and melodic guitar-driven arrangements.

The album is often characterized by:

- Simplicity
- Emotional honesty
- Reflection
- Longing
- Romantic themes
- Personal introspection
