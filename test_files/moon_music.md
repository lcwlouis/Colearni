# Moon Music

## Overview

Moon Music is Coldplay's tenth studio album.

It continues ideas introduced in Music of the Spheres while placing greater emphasis on:

- Love
- Optimism
- Connection
- Environmental awareness
- Shared humanity

The album presents a hopeful outlook on life and relationships.
