# Everyday Life

## Overview

Everyday Life is the eighth studio album by Coldplay.

The album was released in 2019 and is divided into two thematic halves:

1. Sunrise
2. Sunset

The project explores themes of:

- Humanity
- Conflict
- Hope
- Religion
- Identity
- Everyday struggles
- Global perspectives

## Album Structure

| Side | Description |
|--------|--------|
| Sunrise | Reflection, uncertainty, conflict |
| Sunset | Resolution, hope, connection |
